## 1. Project Overview

### Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Project Name** | iFranchise |
| **Brand Tagline** | India's Trusted Franchise Growth Platform |
| **Version** | 0.0.0 (active development) |
| **Type** | SaaS + Consulting + Marketplace Hybrid |
| **Primary Market** | India — Bangalore HQ, Pan-India reach |
| **Aesthetic Target** | Stripe + Framer + Linear quality |

### Vision

iFranchise is not a directory. It is a **strategic growth ecosystem** — a platform where franchise brands, investors, and expansion leaders converge to build scalable, data-driven business empires. The website must communicate authority, trust, and premium execution at every scroll position.

### Mission

To become India's most trusted franchise intelligence platform by combining verified opportunity listings, investor-grade positioning tools, and founder-first consulting services into one unified digital experience.

### Target Users

| User Type | Goal | What the Site Gives Them |
|---|---|---|
| **Franchise Investors** | Find verified, high-ROI opportunities | Curated listings, ROI data, brochure downloads |
| **Franchise Brands** | Expand to new markets | Visibility, lead generation, partner matching |
| **Founders / Operators** | Scale their business | Strategy services, consulting CTAs, contact forms |
| **Expansion Leaders** | Evaluate franchise models | FOCO/FOFO/FICO breakdowns, financial tables |

### Business Model

- **Lead Generation** — Contact forms and CTAs feed a CRM pipeline for franchise consulting sales
- **Franchise Listings** — Brands pay for premium placement and verified badge status
- **Consulting Services** — Strategy, expansion, investor positioning sold as service packages
- **Brochure Downloads** — PDF lead capture per franchise brand (`brochureUrl` field per entry)
- **Future SaaS** — Investor dashboard, live database, booking system (see Section 16)

### Strategic Positioning

Every design decision reinforces: *this company operates at an investor-grade level*. The aesthetic is premium monochrome with violet/indigo accents, glassmorphism, and motion that communicates intelligence rather than decoration. The site is deliberately NOT a template — it is a custom-built product.
