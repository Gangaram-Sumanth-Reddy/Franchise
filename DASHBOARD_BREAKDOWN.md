## 💼 FOR INVESTORS DASHBOARD (Left Side)
**Target Audience:** Investors looking for franchise opportunities to invest in

---

### 1️⃣ **Header Section**
**Component:** "Opportunity Discovery" with Live pill
- **What it shows:** Dashboard title with live status
- **Purpose:** Shows this is a real-time opportunity finder
- **Subtitle:** "24 Verified Franchises"
- **Meaning:** There are 24 verified franchise opportunities available for investment

---

### 2️⃣ **Filter Tags (4 Buttons)**

#### Buttons:
1. **All** - Shows all opportunities (default, purple)
2. **F&B** - Food & Beverage franchises only
3. **Retail** - Retail franchises only
4. **Tech** - Technology franchises only

#### Purpose:
- Allows investors to filter opportunities by industry
- **Interactive:** Click to filter cards below
- **No redirect:** Filtering happens within the dashboard
- **Active state:** Selected filter is highlighted in purple

#### How it works:
- Click "F&B" → Shows only Food & Beverage opportunities
- Click "Retail" → Shows only Retail opportunities
- Click "All" → Shows mixed opportunities

---

### 3️⃣ **Opportunity Cards (3 Cards)**
**Component:** Clickable franchise opportunity cards

#### Each Card Shows:

**Card Structure:**
- **Icon:** Industry-specific icon (Coffee, Activity, Tool, Shopping Bag)
- **Name:** Franchise brand name (e.g., "Premium Coffee Chain")
- **Checkmark badge:** ✓ Verified
- **Industry:** Category (F&B, Wellness, Technology, Retail)
- **ROI:** Return on Investment (e.g., "+32% ROI")
- **Investment:** Minimum investment required (e.g., "Min: $250K")
- **Arrow:** Indicates clickable/interactive

#### Example - Premium Coffee Chain Card:
- **Icon:** Coffee cup (FiCoffee)
- **Name:** Premium Coffee Chain ✓
- **Industry:** F&B
- **ROI:** +32% ROI
- **Investment:** Min: $250K
- **Purpose:** Shows a verified franchise opportunity in Food & Beverage
- **Meaning:** Invest minimum $250K to get 32% return on investment
- **Action:** Click to view full details on opportunities page

#### Cards Change Based on Filter:
- **All:** Shows Coffee, Fitness, Tech Repair
- **F&B:** Shows Coffee Chain
- **Retail:** Shows Fashion Boutique
- **Tech:** Shows Tech Repair Franchise

## 🎯 KEY DIFFERENCES BETWEEN DASHBOARDS

### FOR BRANDS (Brand Owners):
- **Focus:** "How is MY franchise network performing?"
- **Shows:** Locations, revenue, growth, geographic spread
- **Purpose:** Track expansion success
- **Action:** Monitor and manage existing franchises

### FOR INVESTORS (Investors):
- **Focus:** "What opportunities can I invest in?"
- **Shows:** Available franchises, ROI, investment amounts, market trends
- **Purpose:** Discover investment opportunities
- **Action:** Find and invest in franchises
